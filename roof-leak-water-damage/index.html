Roof Leak Water Damage in Springfield: What to Know

Where you see the stain and where the rain gets in are usually two different places. A loose shingle, a failed flashing joint or an aging seal around a skylight lets rain in, and it travels across the roof deck and framing until it falls into the insulation. A ceiling stain tends to arrive late, well after the space overhead first got wet. We find the full wet area and dry it out.

We are a water damage crew, not a roofing company. Our job is the inside of the house: pulling out soaked insulation, drying the framing and drywall, treating for mold and putting the ceiling back together. Should the leak still be active, our crew can cover that section with a tarp for now, or time our work with the roofing contractor you pick so nothing gets sealed over an open leak.

Do This First

What to Do Right Now

  1. Shut Off Wet CircuitsWater coming through a ceiling fan, a light or close to an outlet means that circuit should be turned off at the panel, and the fixture should not be touched.
  2. Catch the DripsSet buckets or pans under active drips and move furniture, rugs and electronics out of the way. Lay towels or plastic sheeting down to protect the floor.
  3. Relieve a Ceiling BulgeA ceiling that sags with trapped water can be drained: stay off to the side and make a small opening at its lowest spot, with a bucket underneath.
  4. Leave the Roof AloneClimbing on slick shingles or crawling around a dark attic is not worth the risk. Note from inside where the water is coming in and leave the rest to professionals.
  5. Document What You SeeUse your phone to record the damaged ceiling, attic and belongings first, hold off on throwing things out until that is done, and call us to get the drying started.
The Usual Culprits

How It Usually Starts

  • Corroded or loose metal flashing where the roof meets a chimney, a vent or a wall.
  • A fallen limb or large hail that punctures or bruises the roof covering.
  • Skylight seals and curbs that dry out, crack and let rain run inside.
  • Popped or exposed roofing nails, each one a tiny opening for rain.
  • Wind-torn, curling or split shingles that leave the underlayment open to rain.
If It Is Left

Where the Damage Spreads

Hidden Attic Mold

A dark, warm attic with damp wood and paper-faced drywall suits mold well. Growth can begin within 24 to 48 hours and spread across the underside of the decking.

Drywall Ceilings

Staining, sagging and loss of strength follow when drywall is not dried. Joints open, tape peels, and a waterlogged piece may fall without much notice.

Ceiling Fixtures and Wiring

Leak water heads for openings in the ceiling, such as recessed lights, fan boxes and junction boxes. Keep that circuit off until someone has inspected it, since damp wiring may corrode or short out.

Wall Cavities

Water that reaches the top of a wall runs down inside it, wetting insulation and the back of the drywall. You might notice nothing more than a peeling patch or a puffy baseboard.

Insurance

Does Insurance Cover It?

Homeowners policies often cover interior water damage when a roof leak is sudden and accidental, such as wind tearing off shingles or a limb puncturing the roof. Insurers generally turn down claims tied to wear, age or lack of upkeep, and they may handle the roof repair on different terms than the inside damage. Photos and moisture readings from us go to the adjuster, though only your policy decides coverage, and your agent can explain it.

How We Fix It

How We Handle Roof Leak Water Damage

1

Map Where Water Went

We inspect the attic, ceilings and wall cavities with moisture meters and thermal imaging to map everywhere the rain traveled, including areas that still look dry from the room below.

2

Cover and Protect

An exposed roof gets covered with a temporary tarp, or we line things up with your roofing contractor. Anything sitting beneath the damage, including the flooring, gets protected too.

3

Remove and Dry

We pull out insulation and drywall that cannot be saved, then run air movers and dehumidifiers in the attic and the rooms below, checking moisture readings until the framing is dry.

4

Treat and Rebuild

An antimicrobial product goes on any open wood that needs it. Once the roof repair is done, we replace insulation, hang and finish new drywall, then prime and paint to match the ceiling.
